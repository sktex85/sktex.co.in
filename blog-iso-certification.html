What is ISO 9001:2015?

ISO 9001:2015 is the international standard that specifies requirements for a quality management system (QMS). Organizations use the standard to demonstrate the ability to consistently provide products and services that meet customer and regulatory requirements.

The standard is based on a number of quality management principles including a strong customer focus, the motivation and implication of top management, the process approach, and continual improvement.

What is ZED Certification?

ZED (Zero Defect Zero Effect) is a certification scheme launched by the Government of India to promote manufacturing excellence and environmental sustainability. It encourages Indian manufacturers to produce goods with zero defects and zero adverse environmental and social effects.

Our ZED certification demonstrates S.K.Enterprise's commitment to sustainable manufacturing practices, environmental responsibility, and social impact. This certification validates our efforts in reducing waste, minimizing environmental footprint, and ensuring zero defects in our textile testing fabrics.

By achieving ZED certification, we align ourselves with the Government of India's vision of making India a global manufacturing hub while maintaining the highest standards of quality and environmental consciousness.
