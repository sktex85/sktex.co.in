About S.K.Enterprise

Premium Textile Solutions Since 1985

A legacy of quality, innovation, and trust in textile manufacturing since 1985. From cotton yarn to a full spectrum of specialty fabrics, we deliver excellence for every industry and application.

Who We Are

It was the year 1985 when Mr. Kirankumar Shah, a man with a vision, started the company Madhuram Traders as a trading house of Cotton Yarn in Dyes Manufacturing Units. After getting overwhelming response due to the quality and timely delivery, a manufacturing unit was set up in the year 1987 for manufacturing of Cotton Hangs. In the year 1991, expansion program was initiated and in 1995 founded another company S.K. Enterprise and introduced Cotton Fabrics in the market. But this was not the end. In year 2000 another major expansion was planned and initiated and then Company was in the market with full range of Cotton Fabrics, Hosiery Fabrics, Nylon Fabrics, Wool Fabrics, Silk Fabrics, Multifibers And All types of Testing Fabrics for Dyes, Chemicals, Pigments, Inks & Auxillaries Manufacturing Units.

Our constant and continuous endeavours, to improve our products, services and efficiency has made us one of the few exporters, offering whole range of Fabrics. The inputs to achieve the present level were hard work, expertise, commitment, efficiency and the much-needed confidence.

We are supplying testing Fabrics for Reactive Dyes, Direct Dyes, Acid Dyes, Basic Dyes, Vat Dyes, Sulfur Dyes, Disperse Dyes, Mordant Dyes, Azoic Dyes, Solvent Dyes, Pigment Paste/Powder, Textile Inks and Textile Auxiliaries.

We believe in quality, integrity, and customer satisfaction above all else.

Quality Assurance

Since inception, quality has been the foremost aspect of our business activities. Our quality controllers have in-depth domain knowledge and they ensure that the production and post production processes are carried out in compliance with the industry standards. They check the raw material used as well as the finished products on well defined parameters before these are delivered to the clients.

Some of the factors based on which we check the range include:
  • Quality of fabrics that’s fits in each of the criteria
  • Consistency in quality of all material
  • Perfection in size And cutting
  • Perfection in weight
  • Long-term use And packing
  • Resistance to weather
The following parameters are checked in quality control section:
  1. Dimensional stability of fabric
  2. Shrinkage of fabric
  3. Color fastness for Washing, Rubbing, Perspiration, Light.
  4. Pilling resistance of fabric
  5. Bursting strength of fabric
  6. GSM testing of fabric.
  7. Sublimation testing.
