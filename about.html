Who We Are

It was the year 1985 when Mr. Kirankumar Shah, a man with a vision, started the company Madhuram Traders as a trading house of Cotton Yarn in Dyes Manufacturing Units. After getting overwhelming response due to the quality and timely delivery, a manufacturing unit was set up in the year 1987 for manufacturing of Cotton Hanks. In the year 1991, expansion program was initiated and in 1995 founded another company S.K. Enterprise and introduced Cotton Fabrics in the market. But this was not the end. In year 2000 another major expansion was planned and initiated and then Company was in the market with full range of Cotton Fabrics, Hosiery Fabrics, Nylon Fabrics, Wool Fabrics, Silk Fabrics, Multifibers And All types of Testing Fabrics for Dyes, Chemicals, Pigments, Inks & Auxillaries Manufacturing Units.

Continuing this legacy of innovation and excellence, Texlab Solutions Private Limited was established in 2026 to grow with your trust and to provide even better quality and service.

Over the years, we have consistently expanded our capabilities to meet the growing demands of our clients. In 2023, we achieved another milestone by establishing a second unit in Vatva, Ahmedabad, strengthening our operations and enhancing our capacity to serve our customers more efficiently. With decades of experience and a focus on continuous progress, we remain committed to delivering excellence and building lasting partnerships with our clients.

Our constant and continuous endeavours, to improve our products, services and efficiency has made us one of the few exporters, offering whole range of Fabrics. The inputs to achieve the present level were hard work, expertise, commitment, efficiency and the much-needed confidence.

We are supplying testing Fabrics for Reactive Dyes, Direct Dyes, Acid Dyes, Basic Dyes, Vat Dyes, Sulfur Dyes, Disperse Dyes, Mordant Dyes, Azoic Dyes, Solvent Dyes, Pigment Paste/Powder, Textile Inks and Textile Auxiliaries.

We believe in quality, integrity, and customer satisfaction above all else.

Why Choose Us

We are one of the leading manufacturers of Laboratory Testing Fabrics for Dyes, Chemicals, Pigments, Inks & Auxillaries Manufacturing Units. From cotton yarn to a full spectrum of specialty fabrics, we deliver excellence for every industry and application.

We are amongst the leading manufacturer and supplier of a wide range of fabrics. Our range of products includes fabrics used for testing different dyes and chemicals. From dyeing to printing, our ranges of fabrics are multi-purpose and applicable in various industries. We are committed to Providing consistent and best quality.

Quality has been at the core of our business since inception. Every stage of our process, from raw material selection to final delivery, is carefully monitored by experienced quality controllers with deep domain knowledge. We follow strict industry standards to ensure consistent excellence in every product.

Our fabrics are checked on key parameters such as dimensional stability, shrinkage, color fastness (against washing, rubbing, perspiration, and light), pilling resistance, bursting strength, GSM testing, and sublimation performance. In addition, we ensure accuracy in size, cutting, weight, long-term durability, and resistance to weather. Every piece—whether cotton, hosiery, nylon, wool, polyester, silk, or hank—is weighed and tested to guarantee perfect consistency.

By choosing us, you choose precision, reliability, and uncompromising quality in every product.
