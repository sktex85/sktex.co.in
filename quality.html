OUR QUALITY

Since inception, quality has been the foremost aspect of our business activities. Our quality controllers have in-depth domain knowledge and they ensure that the production and post production processes are carried out in compliance with the industry standards. They check the raw material used as well as the finished products on well defined parameters before these are delivered to the clients.

Some of the factors based on which we check the range includes the following:

  • Quality of fabrics that's fits in each of the criteria
  • Consistency in quality of all material
  • Perfection in size And cutting
  • Perfection in weight
  • Long-term use And packing
  • Resistance to weather
